How wear is priced. Throughput: every kWh in or out costs cell value ÷ (2 × cycle life). Cycle depth (Xu et al., 2018): the battery is split into five energy slices and deeper slices cost more, so shallow cycles are cheap and deep ones dear. Both stay linear programs.

How every case is scored. The same yardstick for all three: rainflow-count the charge trace, apply life used per cycle = depth1.5 ÷ 6,000, add calendar ageing over a 15-year calendar life, and convert to euros of cells used up.

What is illustrative. The ageing curve is an LFP-like order of magnitude, not a fitted cell; there is no temperature or C-rate effect yet. The site is fictional; its heat and heat-pump profiles come from When2Heat.

The model. HEXOS, a Pyomo / HiGHS mixed-integer program for a site with solar, a CHP engine, a heat pump, a heating rod, a heat store and this battery, solved as 365 overlapping 48-hour windows.
